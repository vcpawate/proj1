Why Roseville Places Community

Placer County · Sacramento Region

Roseville is one of California's best-kept gems.

Sunny valley skies, top-rated schools, a thriving job market, and a genuinely friendly community — Roseville pairs small-town warmth with big-city convenience, just 20 minutes from downtown Sacramento and an hour from Lake Tahoe.

See what makes it special

Why people love living here

A city that keeps showing up on "best places to live" lists — and for good reason.

☀️

Great weather, year-round

Over 260 sunny days a year with mild winters, making it easy to enjoy the outdoors any month of the year.

🎓

Excellent schools

Roseville City and Dry Creek school districts, plus Roseville Joint Union High School District, are consistently among the region's top performers.

💼

A booming economy

Home to major employers like HP Inc., Kaiser Permanente, and Sutter Health, Roseville anchors one of Northern California's fastest-growing job markets.

🛍️

Shopping & dining for days

The Westfield Galleria at Roseville is one of the largest shopping centers in Northern California, surrounded by a great mix of restaurants and entertainment.

🌳

Parks and open space

Miles of scenic trails along Miners Ravine and Dry Creek, plus Maidu Regional Park and its Nature Center, keep the outdoors close to home.

🚗

Unbeatable location

Twenty minutes to downtown Sacramento, under an hour to the Sierra foothills, and about ninety minutes to Lake Tahoe skiing.

Places worth exploring

Historic Old Town / Vernon Street

Charming brick storefronts, seasonal festivals, and a genuine small-town Main Street feel right in the middle of a growing city.

The Fountains at Roseville

An open-air lifestyle center with boutique shops, restaurants, and a splash fountain that's a favorite spot for families on warm afternoons.

Folsom Lake & the American River

Boating, paddleboarding, hiking, and cycling are all just a short drive away — Roseville puts the best of the outdoors within easy reach.

Community that feels like home

Safe neighborhoods, active civic life, and a calendar full of things to do.

  • Consistently ranked among the safest cities of its size in California
  • Award-winning Roseville Utility Exploration Center and one of the largest municipally owned electric utilities in the state
  • Golfland Sunsplash, the Roseville Aquatics Complex, and Mahany Park keep families entertained all year
  • Easy Amtrak and freeway access via I-80 puts the whole region within reach
  • A strong sense of community, from farmers markets to summer concert series
